## Why

The web pod is moving to CLIProxyAPI through `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` and `ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN`. Today doctor reads that token as per-token Anthropic billing and warns. Behind a gateway, the token authenticates to the gateway, and the gateway's own credential decides billing.

## What changed

- `checkBillingSource` reads `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` after the cloud switches. A host other than `api.anthropic.com` passes with the gateway host. A value that does not parse warns, and the warning does not print the value.
- `deploy/README.md` documents the proxy pair as exclusive with `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN`. Section 2b of `docs/RUNNING_ON_A_BUDGET.md` explains the gateway case.
